# stress, de-stress, drawn out, shortened
demo.07  Ava: I [never](+) said it was [your](-) fault. I said it was [somebody's](++).

# pitch: a shift, then a shape across two words
demo.09  Sam: Really? [Really](^2>-1)? You came [all the way](^0>3>0) up here for that?
# pauses after a word, and a silent word
demo.10  Ava: Well, [um](=)... the [light](|||) is still working.
# say different words than the text shows
demo.11  Narrator: She checked her watch: [9:40](=twenty to ten), [approx.](=approximately) on time.
